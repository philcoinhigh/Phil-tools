An OTC desk written in Phil

A maker lists tokens with a desk at a price per token. Round by round a taker buys some, or passes. A listing ends when it sells out, expires, or the maker cancels. With several listings, a book offers the cheapest one first.

Tokens and cash are linear values in the program, so Phil rejects any desk or book that keeps, copies or loses one. Set the terms and run the real program below.

Clip Bounties.

Post a challenge with the prize locked on chain. Players enter with a link to their clip. The best one gets paid, and the prize can't be pulled back.

Looking is free. A wallet is only needed to post, enter or pay out.

Challenges

Reading the board from Solana…

Post a challenge

The prize leaves your wallet now and is locked. You pick the winner. If you don't pick in time, anyone can start a draw and a block hash picks an entry. You only get it back if nobody enters.

Challenge

    Entries

      Post the clip on X, YouTube, TikTok, Twitch or similar, then paste its link. Your entry is timed and numbered on chain. Storing it costs a small amount of test SOL.

      How a prize moves

      1. Locked when posted

      Phil's upload server seals the challenge, and the program refuses it unless the prize is already inside. No prize, no challenge.

      Phil + ours
      2. Entries are sealed

      Each link goes through Phil's server too, and gets a number, a time and its wallet. Nobody can enter as someone else or edit an entry.

      Phil + ours
      3. Paid exactly once

      The sponsor picks, or after the deadline a block hash nobody chose draws. Either way the prize goes to an entrant's own wallet, once.

      ours

      The same rule, in Phil

      A prize is something that must be paid once: not twice, and not zero times. Phil can say that in the type, and then refuses to compile a program that pays it twice.

      Before you rely on it

      • Test coins only. This is on Solana devnet and nobody independent has reviewed it. Do not lock anything of value.
      • The lock is not a judge. It guarantees the prize leaves for an entrant. It cannot tell whether a clip is real, whose it is, or whether the sponsor picked fairly. A sponsor could even enter from a second wallet and pick themselves.
      • The draw can be crowded. Anyone can enter more than once from different wallets, which raises their odds if it comes to a draw. The sponsor's pick is the main path; the draw only exists so a prize can't be withheld.
      • Whoever produces the drawing block has a little influence over its hash, as with any block-hash draw.
      • What is Phil, and what is ours. Phil's compiled server checks that each challenge and entry arrived exactly as it was sealed. Locking, picking, drawing and paying are our code, are not Phil, and are not verified.
      • Links only. Clips stay wherever you posted them, under that site's rules and the game's own rules for sharing footage. Entries are public and permanent.
      • Entry is free apart from network costs. Prize contests have rules that differ by country; whoever sponsors one is responsible for following them.
